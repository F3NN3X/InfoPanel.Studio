A small pill at the top of the kiosk that shows the time, and expands to show alerts and notices.

## Dynamic Island

Open **Dynamic Island** on the left rail and turn on **Show Dynamic Island**. The panel has these sections, which open and close like the sections of the inspector on the right:

| Section | Holds |
|---|---|
| Shape | Style (Pill or Notch), Size (S, M or L), Scale, Offset from top, Reserve space |
| Idle | **When idle**: Time, Time and date, Time and values, Line or Hidden; Rotate every |
| Look | Color (Black, Theme or Custom), Opacity, Border, Shadow, Font, Weight |
| Clock | Seconds and Hours |
| Motion | Animation: Lively, Smooth or Off |
| Alerts | Collapse alerts after, Group notices |
| Dashboard notices | Page name, Layout updated, Plugin back, On air (OBS), Calendar countdown, Coding agents |

Press **Preview an alert** to see how an alert looks. The panel also lists your widgets with a **Send to island** switch each, so a widget can raise its own alerts. A widget's thresholds and island switch are under **Behaviour**.

## Alerts

Open **Alerts** on the left rail. **Alerts on** turns the rules on or off. The panel has the sections **Active now** (what is firing), **History**, **Rules** and **Global defaults**, which open and close like the inspector's. Each rule has an on/off toggle, **Duplicate rule** and **Delete rule**, and shows when it **Last fired**. The **+** menu adds a **Blank sensor rule**, a **Blank notification rule** or a rule from **Presets**.
