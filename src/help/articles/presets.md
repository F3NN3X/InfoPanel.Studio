Starting points you can drop in and adjust: whole pages, single panels, and looks.

| Kind | Is | Find it |
|---|---|---|
| Layout starters | One sensor-free panel; each widget asks for a sensor | **Add › Layouts** |
| Panel presets | One panel already bound to sensors (samples) | **Add › Panels** |
| Panels | Panels you saved | **Add › Panels** (under My panels), **Library › Panels** |
| Styles | One widget's look | Style tab, **Start from a Style**, **Library › Styles** |
| Saved themes and template themes | A whole look for All screens, a screen or a page | **Theme › Switch theme** |
| Skins and palettes | One part of a theme | The **Skin** and **Palette** rows of the theme card in **Theme** |

## Skins

A skin is how every widget is drawn. Pick one on the **Skin** row of the theme card in Theme, for All screens, a screen or a page, or per widget in its Style tab. Picking one is a single undo step and keeps the colors, corners, background and fine-tune.

:::generated presets.appearance

## Saved themes and palettes

**Save as theme** on a custom look's card, or **+ Save current look as a theme** in **Switch theme**, keeps the level's palette, skin, corners, background and fine-tune. **Switch theme** applies a saved theme or a template's look to All screens, a screen or a page; turn on **Include background** to replace the background too. On a page a theme brings its skin and fine-tune only. Rename, duplicate or delete a saved theme from its **⋯** there.

**+ Save current colors as a palette** in the Palette picker keeps the 19 color tokens as a palette of your own. See [Themes](themes).
