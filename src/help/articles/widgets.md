## Adapting to size

Most widgets pick a layout from the space they get: **standard**, **card**, **compact** or **simple**. In Show › Fit, **Small zones** decides what happens when the zone is small:

- **Auto** — the widget decides.
- **Full** — always the full layout, scaled down.
- **Card** — a summary card with the key value.
- **Dense** — everything, packed tighter.

## Hide when idle

Spotify, Media, Steam, FPS and Notifications can hide themselves when there is nothing to show (no music, no game). Turn it on in Behaviour.

## Tint

Spotify and Media can tint their surface from the cover art, Weather from the conditions, Steam from the game banner. Find it in Style › Widget colors.
