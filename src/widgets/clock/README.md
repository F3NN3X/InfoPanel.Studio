# Clock

The time and date.

## What it shows

The time with seconds and the date, as a digital, analog, line, figure or stacked face (the time over the short
date). The title only, no header bar; small zones get a dense view of their own.

## Data it needs

The clock plugin, `plugins.clock-plugin.clock.*` (it owns the `plugins.clock-plugin.` prefix): `24-hour`, `12-hour`,
`minute`, `second`, `day`, `month` and `year`. It draws without sensor keys too.

## Options

- **Face**: Digital, Analog, Line, Figure or Stacked.
- **Seconds** and **Date**.
- Units & format: the clock format (24 h unless set; Auto follows the date locale).
