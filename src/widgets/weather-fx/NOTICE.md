Based on web-weather by greywen (https://github.com/greywen/web-weather, commit 69ec744ffaafee2483ae997c0689129609102816), MIT licence as stated in its README.

Ported from it: the canvas particle systems (rain and splashes, snowfall and snow pile, hail, lightning, fog puffs, wind streaks), the sun glow and the cloud layers. The sound code is not used.
